# Authors

## Daniel Alfocea (cr0hn)

Copyright holder of ByteBazaar / vulnerable-python. The MIT terms and a short bio also
appear in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

**Daniel Alfocea**, known in security as **cr0hn**, has worked in cybersecurity
and software development for over twenty years. He began on Telefónica R&D's
ethical hacking team, later led API security research and product work at
42Crunch, and specializes in critical security, REST APIs, and high-scale
systems.

He maintains a large open source footprint (100+ tools). Projects such as
[Dockerscan](https://github.com/cr0hn/dockerscan),
[FestIn](https://github.com/cr0hn/festin),
[Enteletaor](https://github.com/cr0hn/enteletaor), and Dockerfile Security have
been packaged in **Kali Linux** and **BlackArch**. He is a regular conference
speaker, founded **Navaja Negra**, and co-founded the **OWASP Madrid** chapter.

He also builds and teaches secure-development workshops for engineering teams:
break a real app, see why the obvious fix fails, then close the bug for real.
ByteBazaar is the open lab behind that approach.
